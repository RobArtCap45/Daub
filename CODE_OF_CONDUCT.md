# Code of conduct

Loom is for designers, artists, students, hobbyists and programmers, and it works best when all of them feel welcome here.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** People arrive with very different backgrounds. A question that looks basic to you may be someone's first step.
- **Assume good intent, and be specific.** Say what is wrong and what would help, not what is wrong with the person.
- **Give credit.** Say where an idea or a piece of art came from.
- **Respect people's time and privacy.** Do not share someone's personal information or private messages.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual attention nobody asked for, threats, deliberately derailing a conversation, and spam. These apply in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any space connected to the project.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or open a private security advisory if you need privacy. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code applies to everyone who takes part, maintainers included.
